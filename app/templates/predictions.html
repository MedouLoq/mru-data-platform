Price Predictions

Gradient-boosting forecasts on 2+ years of BCM and Yahoo Finance prices, benchmarked against a naive forecast

Each model predicts the next-day return from 18 scale-free features (past returns, distance to moving averages, volatility, calendar) and is compared with the naive forecast “tomorrow = today”. A skill below 0 % means the naive forecast was more accurate: treat forecasts as scenarios, not advice. Models are validated with 5-fold time-series CV.
